Fetch at generation time, save the snapshot alongside the week's data so a re-run reproduces the same grade, and label it with its fetch date. If the fetch fails, the edition proceeds with an unavailable entry rather than failing.\n\n2. PICK TIERS ARE ESTIMATES. A pick's tier depends on where its original team finishes.
