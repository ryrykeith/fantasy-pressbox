description: "Report each surviving team's count of rostered starters on bye for the next few weeks — carved out of \"Chopping-block and danger analysis\" because the data that task assumed exists does not.\n\nWHAT WAS FOUND\nThat task's description (mine, and wrong) claimed \"the data to compute it exists in the player records.\" It does not. The cached /players/nfl dictionary (data/cache/players-nfl.json, 12,228 players) was inspected directly — every top-level key across every player, plus a raw-text scan for \"bye\" at any nesting depth. No bye-week field exists. Verified separately against https://docs.sleeper.com/: Sleeper has no bye-week endpoint, no schedule endpoint, and no per-team weekly opponent data. Sleeper cannot answer this question.\n\nTHE APPROACH — derive byes from the NFL schedule, then map player -> NFL team -> bye\nA bye is not a published field anywhere; it is the absence of a game. Take a week's NFL schedule, and any team with no game that week is on bye. Each roster's starters already resolve to an NFL team via normalizePlayer, so team-level byes give player-level byes.\n\nVERIFIED, NOT ASSUMED\nESPN's public scoreboard endpoint carries the schedule and was tested against real responses:\n\n  https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/scoreboard?seasontype=2&week=<1-18>&dates=<season>\n\nWalking weeks 1-18 of 2025 and collecting competitors produced exactly 32 teams, each with exactly one missing week — a clean one-bye-per-team result with no gaps or duplicates. The 2026 season returns the same shape (week 6: 14 games, 28 teams playing, 4 on bye), so the schedule is published far enough ahead to be useful in-season, not just historically.\n\nTWO MAPPING GOTCHAS THAT WILL SILENTLY CORRUPT THE COUNT\nSleeper's team abbreviations and ESPN's do not fully agree. Compared directly against this project's cached player blob:\n- Washington is WAS in Sleeper and WSH in ESPN. Unaliased, every Washington starter looks like it has no bye.\n- Sleeper still carries OAK on retired players (the Raiders are LV now). OAK has no current-season schedule and must not be treated as a missing bye.\nAn unmapped or unknown abbreviation must be refused loudly, never silently treated as \"no bye\" — a team that appears to never have a bye is exactly the kind of wrong fact that reaches print.\n\nDESIGN — generate once into config, do not add a runtime dependency\nThis project has zero dependencies and talks to exactly one third party. Prefer a small script (e.g. scripts/fetch-bye-weeks.mjs) that walks the schedule once per season and writes a reviewable config/bye-weeks.<season>.yml of nflTeam -> week, which is committed. Runtime reads only that file, the way it already reads config/guillotine.yml's declared eliminations.\n\nRationale: the table is 32 static rows that never change once the season's schedule is published, so fetching it on every run buys nothing and puts an undocumented third-party API on the critical path of every edition. A checked-in table is inspectable, diffable, and cannot fail at 9pm on a Sunday. Deriving it beats hand-entering it; depending on it at runtime does not.\n\nDo NOT hardcode a season's bye schedule in src/. Byes shift every year, and CLAUDE.md's rule against hard-coded league-specific values rules it out.\n\nWHAT TO COMPUTE\n- Per surviving team, the count of rostered STARTERS (not full roster) on bye in each of the next few weeks. league.startingSlots plus each roster's starterIds identify starters; normalizePlayer already resolves a player id to its NFL team.\n- Numbers only, in src/analysis/ — alongside the other guillotine danger facts in src/analysis/danger.mjs, or its own module if the bye source needs its own loader/validator. Never prose, consistent with src/analysis/week.mjs and danger.mjs.\n\nACCEPTANCE\n- Bye weeks are derived from the real NFL schedule, not hand-entered and not hardcoded in src/.\n- The generated table covers all 32 teams with exactly one bye each; a season that does not produce that shape fails loudly rather than shipping a partial table.\n- WAS/WSH is aliased and OAK is handled; an unknown abbreviation is refused, never silently treated as no bye.\n- Upcoming bye exposure (rostered starters on bye) is reported per surviving team for the next few weeks, as numeric facts.\n- A missing or malformed config entry is refused at load, mirroring parseDeclaredEliminations in src/analysis/elimination.mjs.\n- docs/architecture.md's \"The danger board\" section is updated to remove the noted gap."
