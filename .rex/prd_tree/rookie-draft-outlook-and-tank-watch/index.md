description: "Give dynasty coverage a view of the coming rookie draft: where each pick is projected to land, who owns it, which prospects headline the class, and which teams are in the race for the top picks.\n\nWHY\nA dynasty trade is partly a bet on a future draft class. The 2027 class is headlined by Ohio State WR Jeremiah Smith (consensus 1.01 in superflex rookie mocks as of 2026-10) and is unusually deep at QB, which matters in this superflex league. Trading DJ Moore for Taco Tuesday' 2027 1st was, in the operator's read, partly a tank for that class. Neither trade grades nor any edition can currently say where a pick will land or what it might buy.\n\nWHAT WAS VERIFIED (2026-10-09)\n- Sleeper does not expose how a rookie draft is ordered: the league's settings carry draft_rounds (5), playoff_teams (6) and pick_trading, and nothing about max points-for or lotteries. The 2027 rookie draft does not exist in Sleeper yet. The rule must therefore be DECLARED, like guillotine format.\n- FantasyCalc prices generic picks by year and tier but carries no college players (Jeremiah Smith is absent). Devy sites have no API and can be stale (KeepTradeCut still listed Smith as 2026-eligible). Prospect knowledge must also be DECLARED, with sources.\n\nTHE OPERATOR'S LEAGUE RULE (verbatim intent)\nPicks 1-6 go to the six teams that missed the playoffs, ordered lowest to highest max points-for. Picks 7-12 go to the six playoff teams, also ordered lowest to highest max points-for. A non-playoff team with a high max PF still picks before any playoff team with a lower one.\n\nTHE STORY THIS ENABLES (live data, 2026-10-09, week 4)\nRebuild Szn held 1.02 (its own) and Taco Tuesday' pick, projected 1.07 because JD was the 6th and last playoff seed, ahead of Sunday Scaries only on points-for. If JD missed the playoffs that pick would jump to 1.02. A pick crossing the playoff line is the central drama of a tank watch in this format."
